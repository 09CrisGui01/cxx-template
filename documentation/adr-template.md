# ADR template

**Purpose:** a lightweight template for architecture decision records (ADRs). Use an ADR for a
significant architectural decision, not for routine implementation details.

Copy this file to `documentation/architecture/decisions/NNNN-short-title.md`, using the next
unused number, fill it in, and add the record to the [documentation index](index.md).

---

# ADR NNNN: Title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Accepted; amended by ADR NNNN | Superseded by ADR NNNN | Deprecated
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Supersedes** or **Amends:** ADR NNNN (optional line; omit when the record stands alone)

## Context

What problem or force makes a decision necessary? State facts and constraints, and say which were
verified.

## Decision

What was decided, in active voice ("We use ...").

## Alternatives considered

Each real alternative and why it was not chosen.

## Consequences and trade-offs

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what must be revisited and when.
