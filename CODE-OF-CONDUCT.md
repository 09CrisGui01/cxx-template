# Code of conduct

This project is a place for technical work. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests,
reviews and discussions, MUST be treated with respect.

## Expected behavior

- Be courteous and professional. Criticize ideas and code, not people.
- Accept review comments and give them with a stated reason.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear.
- Keep discussions on the topic of the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing private information about another person without permission.
- Repeated off-topic or disruptive comments after a maintainer has asked you to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers MAY edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who
break these rules. Report a problem to {{contact-email}}. Reports are handled in confidence.

Replace `{{contact-email}}` with a real address when you create a project from this template.
